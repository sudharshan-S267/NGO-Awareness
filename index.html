About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving lives through meaningful social initiatives. The organization works across education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, community service and youth skill development.

Through volunteer-driven projects and awareness campaigns, InAmigos Foundation continues to create opportunities and support communities across India.
